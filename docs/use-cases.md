# Use Cases

[English](use-cases.md) · [中文](../zh/use-cases.md)

Who is **Speak Helper** for? Anyone who wants to **listen instead of read**, or who needs **audio help** to access text on screen.

## Listen instead of reading

- **End of a long day** — too tired to read; copy a paragraph and listen.
- **Long articles or docs** — hear content chunk by chunk without scrolling every line.
- **Notifications and chat** — copy messages from WeChat, Slack, email clients, etc.
- **Dense interfaces** — small fonts in legacy apps; select → hotkey → hear clearly.

## Vision and reading support

Speak Helper helps people who **have difficulty seeing or reading on-screen text**, within the scope of **selected or copied content**:

| Audience | How Speak Helper helps |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Low vision** | Hear text from any app after copy or `Ctrl+Alt+R` on selection |
| **Eye strain / screen fatigue** | Shift from reading to listening for part of the day |
| **Older adults** | Simple flow: copy text → confirm bubble → listen; family can configure once |
| **Dyslexia / reading difficulty** | Audio alongside text reduces decoding load |
| **Temporary injury** | Sprained wrist, migraine — less need to stare at screen |

**Important:** This is **not** a replacement for professional **screen readers** (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver). Those navigate the whole UI. Speak Helper only speaks text **you bring to it** (clipboard, selection, OCR from images). Many users pair both: screen reader for navigation, Speak Helper for quick read-aloud of arbitrary snippets.

## Productivity and learning

| Scenario | Workflow |
|----------|----------|
| **Multitasking** | Listen while coding, cooking, walking |
| **Language learning** | Edge TTS / custom voices for Chinese, English, dialects |
| **Research** | Copy quotes from PDFs or web pages and hear them |
| **Image / screenshot text** | Copy image → OCR → TTS (menus, slides, photos of documents) |

## When Speak Helper is a good fit

- You already **see** the text but prefer **audio** (convenience, fatigue).
- You need **occasional** read-aloud, not full-time UI narration.
- You work across **many apps** (copy/hotkey works everywhere).
- You want **free Edge TTS** or your own **OpenAI-compatible** voice API.

## When to use something else

- **Full blind / low-vision navigation** → dedicated screen reader.
- **Continuous narration of entire web pages without copying** → browser read-aloud extensions or screen readers.
- **Offline-only, no network ever** → pre-recorded audio; Edge/OpenAI backends need internet for synthesis.
